What this document is for

The curriculum comparison guide maps all 40 Victorian content descriptions to their Australian equivalents. It does not say which resources teach which code. This does.

Use it to answer three questions a faculty leader will ask: what does this unit actually cover, what does it not cover, and what would an interstate colleague need to change.

1. What this unit covers — and what it does not

This is a water safety unit, not a whole HPE program. It addresses 10 of the 40 Victorian Curriculum 2.0 content descriptions across Levels 7–8 and 9–10. Stating that plainly is more useful than implying blanket coverage.

Read this before using the unit for compliance

Victorian Curriculum 2.0 reports against achievement standards, not content descriptions. The codes below record what was taught. They are not, by themselves, evidence of student achievement — that comes from the assessment rubrics and work samples.

All 12 focus areas must be addressed in each band. This unit services Safety substantially, and contributes to Lifelong physical activities, Challenge and adventure activities and Mental health and wellbeing. It does not cover the other eight. Your program needs them elsewhere.

5. For schools outside Victoria

The AC v9.0 column above gives the substitution for each code. Three things to carry across with it:

  • The movement codes are offset by one. Victoria has ten movement descriptions per band; the Australian Curriculum has nine. A straight number-for-number substitution will be wrong. The column above already accounts for it.
  • First aid and CPR are Victorian additions. VC2HP8P08 and VC2HP10P08 name them explicitly; the Australian descriptions do not. You may keep that content, but it is not required of you.
  • Aquatic settings are named in the Victorian movement codes and not in the Australian ones. This is the curriculum-level basis for Victoria’s mandated swimming and water safety requirement.

The full code-by-code comparison, including the descriptions this unit does not teach, is in the Victorian 2.0 vs Australian Curriculum 9.0 guide. A localisation guide for swapping Victorian statistics, organisations and legislation is in the Australian Curriculum alignment guide.

6. How this map was built

Generated by scanning the published body text of every resource in the suite for Victorian Curriculum codes, then validating each against the VCAA Version 2.0 curriculum export. Codes appearing only in page metadata were excluded, as were the reference documents, which cite codes without teaching them.

Victorian wording is verbatim from the VCAA export. Australian wording and the offset mapping come from the published Years 7–10 sequence of content descriptions.

Regenerate this map rather than hand-editing it if resources are added or recoded.
