THE PEDAGOGICAL CHANGE - READ THIS
-----------------------------------
The Year 7-8 lessons were built on the 5E model: Engage, Explore, Explain,
Elaborate, Evaluate. 5E is an inquiry model in which students explore BEFORE
the teacher explains.

VTLM 2.0 asks for the reverse. Explicit teaching sits at its core and the
department states it is the most effective approach for most students,
including priority cohorts.

Three of the five lessons genuinely ran Explore before Explain. Those have
been resequenced. The activities themselves barely changed - they were
already directed tasks with set timings, not open investigation. What
changed is the order, the phase names, and a new retrieval phase.

New lesson sequence, every lesson:
   1. Review - retrieval practice        3 min   (NEW)
   2. Focus the learning                 5-10 min (was Engage)
   3. Explicit teaching - I do           8-15 min (was Explain, moved earlier)
   4. Guided practice - we do            12-15 min (was Explore)
   5. Independent application - you do   8-30 min (was Elaborate)
   6. Monitor progress                   5-12 min (was Evaluate)

Lesson 3 was left in its original content order: the CPR demonstration
still comes before throw rescue, because both were already teacher-led
demonstrations and reversing them would have broken the teaching sequence.

The Year 9-10 teacher guide was never built on 5E - it uses Know /
Understand / Do, which already sits within the model. It was not changed.

RETRIEVAL PRACTICE - THE REAL GAP
----------------------------------
Across five sequential lessons the unit had no retrieval practice, no
spaced review and no lesson-opening recap of prior learning. "Retention and
recall" is one of VTLM 2.0's four elements of learning.

Lessons 2-5 now open with a three-minute cumulative retrieval starter -
questions from earlier lessons, no notes, self-marked, ungraded. Each
carries a short note explaining to the teacher why it is there.

STEP 2 - Rebuild WaterSafety.zip
--------------------------------
Not included - the combined download would exceed the transfer limit.
After copying both folders into one, Shift + right-click inside it, choose
"Open PowerShell window here", and paste:

    Remove-Item WaterSafety.zip -Force -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
    $files = Get-ChildItem -File | Where-Object {
      ($_.Extension -in '.html','.pdf','.pptx','.docx' -or
       $_.Name -in 'LICENSE','README.md','DATA_PROVENANCE.md') -and
      $_.Name -notlike 'google*' -and $_.Name -notlike 'yandex*'
    }
    Compress-Archive -Path $files.FullName -DestinationPath WaterSafety.zip
    "{0} files, {1:N1} MB" -f $files.Count, ((Get-Item WaterSafety.zip).Length/1MB)

Expect 100 files and about 18.3 MB. Upload it with everything else.
